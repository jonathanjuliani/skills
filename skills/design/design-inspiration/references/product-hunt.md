## Taken

- The feed is the product: rank, name, tagline, category tags, upvote count, comment count. Selecting a launch opens detail without changing the list's visual language.
- Upvote and discussion counts sit on the row so social proof is comparable while scanning, not buried after navigation.
- Day or topic framing (launches, categories) is navigation over the same list pattern rather than a second layout system.
- Marketing modules stay out of the primary scan path. Discovery work needs consecutive rows, not a hero between every item.

## Rejected

- Treating Product Hunt's orange identity or mascot as a brand to import.
- Replacing the ranked row with equal feature cards. Ranking is the decision structure.
- Autoplaying media in the list. Motion that interrupts triage is noise for this job.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing a ranked catalog or launch list that opens into detail. Wrong for dense financial tables or spatial canvas tools.
