## Taken

- Text-led claim first ("Your shortcut to everything"), then proof panels for extensions and AI, so the grid earns its place after the point is clear.
- Extension panels are unequal and concrete (Linear, Notion, Spotify, 1Password) with one-line jobs, not generic "integrations" wallpaper.
- Keyboard and ergonomics are demonstrated (F-keys, hotkeys, snippets) because the audience lives in shortcuts.
- One primary download/get path; community and docs are secondary.
- Reliability is a number (crash-free rate) where it reduces fear for daily drivers.

## Rejected

- Turning every extension into an equal logo wall. Named tools with jobs transfer; logos alone do not.
- Purple glow launcher aesthetics as a theme to copy.
- Multiple competing CTAs for AI, Store, and Download at the same weight.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing a toolkit with a panel grid of real capabilities. Wrong for dense financial tables or single-slot booking pages.
