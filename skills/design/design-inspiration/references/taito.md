## Taken

- Headcount band (roughly 30 to 300) states who the product is for before feature theatre.
- Live assistant and Slack threads show the product doing work, not an illustrated metaphor.
- Capability list is job-shaped (directory, time off, attendance, docs, permissions, pre-payroll) with one sentence each.
- Regional compliance examples (UK, Sweden, Germany) turn policy into concrete rows instead of a vague "global" claim.
- Pricing is one tier, stated plainly, which matches a buyer tired of quote-only HRIS.

## Rejected

- Exact brand illustration and wordmark identity.
- Annual-review theatre as the only HR story. Continuous performance grounded in real tools is the transferable idea.
- Equal audience cards without ranking. Founders, people leaders, and operators should stay ranked by job.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing AI people-ops or HR tooling for growth-stage teams. Wrong for browser support tables or cinematic film portfolios.
