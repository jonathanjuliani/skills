## Taken

- First paint is the layout. Grey blocks match card, title, and meta so the page does not jump when photographs arrive. The wait explains density, it does not hide it.
- Photography is the ranking. Each card is one stay: image, place name, dates, price, rating. Hue is reserved for the search action, not for decoration on the grid.
- Images fill the skeleton in place. Progressive media is a substitution, not an entrance animation on every card.
- Skip to content is present. A marketplace that is mostly images still offers a way past the chrome.

## Rejected

- The wordmark, coral search control, and "Guest favorite" badge as identity.
- Shimmer on every skeleton at once. Layout reservation transfers; a page that sparkles while it loads does not.
- Cookie modal covering listing photography. Consent should not sit on the inventory the user came to scan.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a grid of media records is the job. Wrong for text-led trust pages, cinematic brand worlds, or focused single-task checkout.
