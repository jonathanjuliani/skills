## Taken

- One editorial promise at the top ("What we're building, fixing, and learning") plus a short scope line so scanners know the feed is engineering context, not lifestyle content.
- Topic chips (Announcement, Onboarding, Release, Security, and peers) filter a long chronological list without burying the latest post.
- Each row carries date, author or team, read time, title, and a one-sentence lede. Metadata stays secondary to the title.
- The latest post is visually primary; older posts stay equal peers in a scannable stack rather than a card grid.
- Tags ride with the entry so a security or release reader can jump without opening every post.

## Rejected

- Lobster asides and brand mascot language as transferable voice.
- Equal marketing cards for every post. Ranking by recency with one featured lead is the structure that transfers.
- Award-theatre motion on a blog index. The page wins by scan speed.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing a product or foundation engineering blog. Wrong for cinematic landing heroes or dense financial market tables.
