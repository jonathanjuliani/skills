## Taken

- Product tabs in the chrome (platform, experience, real estate) are wayfinding for a multi-product company. The visitor can see there is more than one intelligence without a hamburger dump.
- Two actions, ranked: Book a call, then See how it works.
- Quotes are attributed to a person and a role, and they point at a named product. Attribution is what makes a quote evidence.
- Outcomes later on the page are specific (units sold, customers, pages of a lease). Numbers beat "insights".
- The page reads as one grotesque plus a mono even though the scrape counted more families. Spacing is 4/8 with hairlines. Medium radius on cards and dialogs; shadows present.

## Rejected

- The office-meeting photograph as a generic stand-in. Real product over a stock room; this photo does not prove the intelligence exists.
- The wordmark and the trial display faces in the scrape. Identity. Do not ship six families because an extractor counted them.
- Autoplaying cards with only a pause note for screen readers. If it moves, the control should be obvious to everyone, not only to AT users.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. A photography hero is the wrong reference for the product UI that answers the question.
