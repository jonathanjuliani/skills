## Taken

- Live rates sit in the hero. For this audience, APY is the product, and a static adjective would be a lie.
- Type on a dark field does the claim. The rest of the chrome is quiet so the number can move.
- Two actions, ranked: Enter App, then Read Docs. The docs path matters in DeFi because due diligence is the job.
- Risk copy is present and numbered. A yield product that hides the disclaimer is not a reference for trust.
- A display face on the claim, a grotesque for the rest. Spacing jumps to large marketing gaps. Modest radius.

## Rejected

- The purple fill on Enter App, and the display family. Identity.
- Huge leftover gaps in the scrape. Those are layout, not tokens.
- Dark cinematic empty space as a brand to copy. The live rate is the idea; the void is decoration.
- Heading levels that are all ones. Hierarchy should be in the outline as well as on the screen.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. A DeFi marketing hero is the wrong reference for a regulated banking UI, even when both show rates.
