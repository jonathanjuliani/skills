## Taken

- The door is a named wait that becomes a still. A full-bleed load word hands off to a portrait, so the transition keeps one person on screen instead of cutting to a new page.
- After the door, scroll is the structure. The portrait and a 3D helmet layer sit in one world; moving through the page is how you leave the still, not a list of equal sections.
- Image and chrome share the wait. The helmet is wireframe evidence that the world is 3D, then the photograph holds. Transfer "load becomes the subject", not a racing timeline.
- First paint was the load word. A long loop that never lands on the portrait would hide the person the site is built to show.

## Rejected

- The lime field, the load word, and the helmet as a layout to copy. Signature choreography.
- Award-theatre chrome around a driver. Ranked story beats transfer; the livery does not.
- Scroll-tied 3D as a default for product UI. This audience entered a world; an operator table does not need a helmet.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a brand or talent page uses a short named door, then a 3D still you scroll through. Wrong for dense tools, forms, or commerce that must be readable without a world.
