## Taken

- Product chrome carries the pitch: review threads, learnings, and pre-merge checks appear as real queues, not illustrated metaphors.
- Triage ranks PRs by priority bands with risk, effort, and ownership metadata so operators can scan a flood.
- Jobs are sequenced (review, prioritize, explain diffs, secure) instead of equal feature tiles.
- Change Stack explains large agent diffs as layered intent, which fits reviewers who need meaning over line noise.
- Specific acceptance metrics and case numbers sit next to claims so proof stays checkable.

## Rejected

- Exact brand wordmark and rabbit mascot identity.
- Letter-spacing gimmicks in the hero as a transferable pattern.
- Equal marketing cards for every agent integration. Ranked operator queues transfer.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing dense engineering review or triage products. Wrong for quiet personal blogs or consumer membership pricing alone.
