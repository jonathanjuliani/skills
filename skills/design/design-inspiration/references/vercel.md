## Taken

- Sections are ranked jobs (agents, apps that scale, platforms for customers), each with a named customer proof and a short capability list.
- "Recently shipped" panels (eve, Passport, Containers) act as an unequal grid of real artifacts, not decorative bento.
- Two actions stay clear: Deploy now versus Talk to sales, ranked for different buyers.
- Copy speaks to both humans and agents without inventing a second site.
- Each capability list is short (four items) so panels stay scannable.

## Rejected

- Dark agentic atmosphere as a mandatory theme.
- Wordmark and black/white identity system to reuse.
- Inflating every panel to equal size with equal CTAs. Ranking by job is the transferable structure.

## Not applicable here

- Store when infrastructure marketing needs ranked panels of capabilities. Wrong for consumer membership pricing or hand-drawn canvases alone.
