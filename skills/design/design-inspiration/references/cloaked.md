## Taken

- The first viewport leads with a threat claim and an immediate action (phone input + Get Started), not a product gallery. Urgency is the structure.
- Antagonists are named as roles (scammers, identity thieves, data brokers) with one concrete consequence each. Specificity beats "we protect your privacy".
- Feature list is outcome-shaped ("Never give out your personal info again", "Remove personal info from 1000+ sites", "$1 million insurance") rather than component names alone.
- Primary conversion stays Get Started; Login is secondary. The page does not offer two equal CTAs.
- Numbers that matter to this audience are counted where they reduce fear (1000+ sites, $1M insurance), not vanity metrics.

## Rejected

- The wordmark and brand illustration language. Identity.
- Fear carousel as a default pattern for every product. It fits privacy threat education; it is wrong for calm B2B tools or dense operators scanning for hours.
- Phone-number capture as the universal first step. That is their acquisition model, not a layout rule.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the brief needs copy-led consumer threat marketing with an inline start action. Wrong reference for product-led developer tools or neutral government services.
