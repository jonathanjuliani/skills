## Taken

- One product, one loop: take a photo → it thinks → it prints a poem. The page demonstrates modes (haiku, limerick, receipt) instead of abstract feature cards.
- Primary action is Buy now; DIY / GitHub is clearly secondary for builders, not a second equal CTA.
- FAQ answers trust blockers for an AI hardware toy (subscription, photo storage, training on data, offline) in the maker's own voice.
- Social proof is named people with roles (poet laureate, Macintosh designer, Anthropic brand), each with one concrete use, not star ratings.
- Open-source DIY is offered without diluting the product story: same idea, different hardware, for workshops and weekends.

## Rejected

- ASCII poem frames and receipt typography as a brand system to reuse. They fit this product; elsewhere they become a gimmick.
- Claiming "tiny self-funded team" as a layout pattern. That is voice and positioning, not a structure to copy.
- Turning every FAQ into a dark-pattern upsell. Their honesty about privacy and subscriptions is the transferable discipline.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the surface is a focused single-product story (buy or DIY). Wrong for multi-product catalogs, dense operator tables, or B2B implementation platforms.
