## Taken

- Progress is shown as product UI. Panels carry a scheduled state, a scroll-depth readout, and a publish overlay, so wait and status explain a job rather than a generic spinner.
- Four unequal panels, each one capability. Ranking by job (build, publish, convert, prove) beats a wall of equal cards.
- Skip to main content is present. A marketing page with moving panels still offers a way past the chrome.
- First paint is type and two ranked actions. The panels can arrive after. The claim does not depend on the demo finishing.

## Rejected

- The wordmark, blue start control, and conference banner as identity.
- Cookie chrome covering the logo strip. Consent should not sit on the first proof.
- Copying a "40% scroll this far" overlay as a signature. The finding is status-in-the-product, not that metric.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a platform wants loading and status to look like the editor. Wrong for cinematic brand worlds or public-sector directories.
