## Taken

- Search is the product: the first viewport is a query field, not a marketing hero.
- Home density uses ranked lists (latest features, most searched, browser scores) so frequent jobs stay one glance away.
- Support legend is explicit (supported, not supported, partial, unknown) and offers a colour-blind-friendly mode.
- Settings expose usage thresholds and personal analytics import so density can be tuned to the reader's audience.
- Related tools and compare routes are listed as jobs, keeping the home a hub rather than a single table dump.

## Rejected

- Marketing illustration or brand theatre on a reference tool. Utility is the craft.
- Equal card grids for features. Ranked lists and a support matrix transfer.
- Colour as the only support signal without a legend and accessible alternative.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing dense developer reference tools. Wrong for cinematic agency landings or membership pricing pages alone.
