## Taken

- Auth steps are the demo. Tabs swap Sign up, Sign in, User button, and Profile in one frame, so a buyer sees how context is kept when the task changes.
- Skip to main content is present. Focused task surfaces that take keyboard seriously advertise it.
- The live component stays a single card. Overlay and step changes happen inside it, which is how a transition explains "you are still in account" rather than dumping the user onto a new layout.
- Copy names the component. Specificity ("pixel-perfect UIs, embedded in minutes") beats a generic "beautiful auth" claim.

## Rejected

- The wordmark, circuit-board hero texture, and purple accent. Identity.
- Copying the exact modal chrome or avatar stack. Reuse step-in-place, not their card.
- A marketing banner competing with the first claim. Rank announcements below the product.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing onboarding, account, or other multi-step focused tasks. Wrong for cinematic brand homes or dense operator tables.
