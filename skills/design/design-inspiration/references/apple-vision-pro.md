## Taken

- Scroll position equals which beat of the story is on screen. A hero photograph of a person wearing the device blurs into overlay type, then a room-scale film, then a closer look at the object. The page is a timeline, not a stack of equal cards.
- Media that the user can stop. The film and the closer-look reel carry pause controls, which is how cinematic scroll stays a product story instead of a trap.
- A local nav appears once you have entered the story (overview, specs, OS, demo, buy). Ranked actions stay available while scroll continues, so cinema does not hide commerce.
- Type over a continuation of the same image. The claim sits on a blurred still of the hero, which keeps one subject while the beat changes.

## Rejected

- The product photography, the wordmark, and the system typeface. Identity.
- Pill CTAs as a commerce signature. Reuse ranked actions, not the capsule.
- Autoplaying a scroll-tied film with no pause on a dense or regulated surface. This audience came to understand a device; operators did not.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a product page uses scroll as a film of one object, with pause and persistent buy paths. Wrong for a records list, a form, or a text-led trust page without a physical product.
