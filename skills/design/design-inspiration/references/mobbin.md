## Taken

- The catalog is the hero. Real app screens, not drawings of apps, because the product is a library of the real thing.
- One primary action (Join for free), with plans as secondary. The search UI appears as soon as the claim is made.
- Scale claims are numbers (apps, screens, flows). The library's size is the reason to trust it.
- Navigation of the sample catalog is named in the language of the work (screens, flows, UI elements), which is what this audience is searching for.
- One family, split in the CSS into weight-named files. Spacing is coarse (large steps). Catalog cards use a large radius; pills are for chips, not for type.

## Rejected

- The looping app-icon carousel as decoration. Motion that does not explain a change becomes noise.
- The wordmark. Identity. Weight-as-family naming in the scrape is not extra families to buy.
- Catalog-card radius as a product chrome to copy. The lesson is "the library is the hero", not the roundness.
- Equal logo rows as a substitute for a named customer outcome. "Trusted by" without a number or a story is wallpaper.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. A pattern-library marketing page is not a brief for the applications inside the library.
