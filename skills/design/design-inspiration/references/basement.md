## Taken

- Mode is a transition. Human and Machine swap the same place rather than opening a second site, so the user keeps orientation when the surface changes.
- Named work is the argument (Vercel, Daylight, KidSuper, MrBeast). Case links sit in the lobby as destinations, not as a logo wall.
- First paint should still be a document. The live read opened on black, then a 3D office. Transfer the mode toggle and named work, not a void while the scene boots.
- Music is opt-in ("Turn music on"). Ambient audio that starts itself is the failure this control is avoiding.

## Rejected

- The 3D office, arcade, and basketball hoop as a layout to copy. Signature world, not a convention.
- Black first paint as a studio default. A hiring manager scanning cases should not wait on a scene.
- The wordmark and "cool shit that performs" as voice to import. Specific shipped work transfers; the slogan does not.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a studio or portfolio needs a mode or route change that keeps place. Wrong for product UI, checkout, or any audience that cannot wait on a 3D lobby.
