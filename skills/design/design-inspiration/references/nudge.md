## Taken

- One primary action (Get in touch). Healthcare buyers are not here to self-serve a checkout.
- A warm, quiet field and a single illustration as the emotional beat, then plain explanation. Restraint reads as care for this audience.
- Use cases are named as clinical jobs (medication adherence, CPAP, recovery), not as generic "engagement". Specificity is how a regulated-adjacent buyer scans.
- A reimbursable-care claim is a labelled chip, not a slogan in the headline. Money and clinical claims stay attached to the evidence.
- One family, two weights. Spacing is a short 4/8 scale with almost no one-offs. Shadows unused. Pills on nav and the primary action; modest radius elsewhere.

## Rejected

- The illustration style (drawn hands). Identity, and distinctive enough to read as theirs on another domain.
- Any palette copied from the paper field or the ink. Those are brand. The named family too.
- Pill-everything as a form pattern. Pills here are marketing chrome, not inputs.
- Carousels of equal use-case cards. Rank the jobs this buyer has; do not imply they are interchangeable.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. A clinic-operator marketing page is the wrong reference for a consumer wellness app or a dense EHR table.
