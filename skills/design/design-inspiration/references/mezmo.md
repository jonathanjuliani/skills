## Taken

- Ownership constraints lead: your infrastructure, your rules, your tools and telemetry. Trust is framed as governance, not vibes.
- Capability sections are outcome-shaped (built for SRE work, governed for production, designed to scale) rather than equal feature tiles.
- A numbered path (pick workflow, run in environment, prove value, expand authority) turns adoption into an operator sequence.
- Live operational chrome (flags, pods, errors) appears as proof the product lives in production context.
- Specific SRE jobs (alert storms, deployment regressions, on-call handoffs) beat generic "AI for ops" claims.

## Rejected

- Lobster or mascot theatre for infrastructure products.
- Dark mesh gradients as a mandatory security aesthetic.
- Inflating every agent persona (green/red/blue style) into equal hero panels. Ranking by job transfers; costume does not.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing governed ops or SRE tooling. Wrong for consumer membership pricing or hand-drawn canvases alone.
