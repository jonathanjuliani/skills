## Taken

- Constraints lead: open source, self-hostable, flat pricing, named certifications. Trust is stated as product facts.
- Core room capabilities (permissions, NDA, watermarking, Q&A, page analytics, audit log) stay as a short scannable contract.
- Agent surfaces (API, CLI, MCP, llms.txt, .md pages) are first-class peers to human docs.
- Audience list is role-shaped (founders, VCs, M&A, legal) so the wrong buyer self-selects out.
- Compare and alternative pages treat category choice as a job, not a buried FAQ.

## Rejected

- Quote-only enterprise opacity as the default for secure sharing. Transparent tiers are the transferable lesson.
- Brand illustration systems as identity to reuse.
- Equal feature cards when a short capability contract already maps the product.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing secure document or data-room products with agent access. Wrong for cinematic film portfolios or browser support tables alone.
