## Taken

- One job in the headline: public URLs for localhost, webhooks, and reviewable snapshots.
- Use cases are concrete (Stripe or Clerk callbacks, share without deploy, comment like Figma) instead of abstract "developer experience".
- Agent paths (MCP and CLI) are listed as the same jobs humans do, not a separate product.
- Pricing contrast is a table of flat seat cost versus metered competitor line items.
- Regional edge claim is one sentence of constraint, not a world map theatre.

## Rejected

- Exact brand wordmark and ornamental glyphs as identity.
- Social-proof tweet walls as the primary structure. Named jobs transfer better.
- Dark cinematic heroes for a utility that wins on clarity.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing focused developer utilities with clear local-to-public workflows. Wrong for dense financial market scanners or editorial magazine archives alone.
