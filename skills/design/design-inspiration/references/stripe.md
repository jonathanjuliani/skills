## Taken

- A live number in the hero (global GDP on the platform). The claim is checkable while you read it, which is why it feels like infrastructure rather than advertising.
- Type is two tones of the same size: the first sentence is the product, the second is the expansion. Hierarchy without a second font. Sans for UI, a mono only for code.
- Spacing is a 4/8 base with hairlines. Corners stay small on controls and cards. Shadows lift surfaces; they are not a glow.
- Two actions, ranked: Get started, then a secondary sign-up path. They share a row but not a weight.
- The rest of the page is products as a map, not three equal feature cards.

## Rejected

- The gradient ribbon. Signature. A colourful wash is also the generated default, which is why it transfers to nothing.
- The wordmark, the purple, and the named families. Identity.
- Half-pixel spacing and hairline radii from the scrape. Dead CSS, not a scale.
- Cookie UI covering the next section. Consent should not sit on the first claim below the fold.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. "Like Stripe" is not a brief. Take the live number and the type restraint, not the gradient, and only if this audience is buying infrastructure.
