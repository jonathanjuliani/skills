## Taken

- Oversized type does the hierarchy. Asides in a quieter size carry the point of view. Two roles for type, not a ladder of six.
- One circular call to talk, and it is the only conversion. Case studies are the content, not competing CTAs.
- Dark field, near-white type, almost no colour. Hue would compete with the words, which are the product.
- Work is listed as named projects with a role and a duration. Specificity about the engagement beats a mood board.
- Weights go down to hairline; that is editorial, not a UI scale. Spacing is irregular. Radius is large because the only conversion is circular.

## Rejected

- The loader that draws a letter in dots. Identity, and it delays the read a hiring manager is doing in seconds.
- The circular button as a motif to copy. One unusual control is theirs; a second site using it is a knockoff.
- The wordmark styling (registered-mark lockup). Identity.
- A zoo of named families and cuts in the scrape. Keep two roles for type, not ten files.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. A personal editorial portfolio is the wrong reference for a SaaS marketing page or an internal tool.
