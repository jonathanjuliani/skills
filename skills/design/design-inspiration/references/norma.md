## Taken

- Photography of the real object in use. A hand, a phone, a scan. The product is proven before features are listed.
- Copy is concrete: a steel disc, one scan, apps disappear until you scan again. No adjectives standing in for the mechanism.
- One primary action (Order), repeated. Secondary paths (How it works, FAQ) are named, not competing buttons.
- Type is few sizes, one family. The headline does the hierarchy; the rest stays quiet so the photo can.
- Spacing is an unusually regular 4/8 scale. Generous radius belongs to product frames, not to every control.
- How-it-works is three short steps, not a feature wall. Sequence is the product.

## Rejected

- The disc photography, the wordmark, and the named family. Those are identity.
- Large radius on product frames as a control scale. It is photography chrome.
- A discount overlay that covers the hero. It fights the photography the page is built on.
- Feature lists that grow into a catalogue of equal items. Rank them, or keep the three-step.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. A physical-goods landing is the wrong reference for a dense admin tool.
