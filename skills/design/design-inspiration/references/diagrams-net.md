## Taken

- The canvas is first paint after load. Shape libraries and style controls dock around an empty board ready for nodes and edges.
- Direct manipulation is the product: place, connect, select, style. Panels exist to serve the selection, not to compete with the board.
- Search for shapes and zoom live at the edges so the spatial artifact keeps most of the viewport.
- Sharing and file concerns stay in chrome outside the drawing surface. The board does not open behind a marketing wall.

## Rejected

- Treating draw.io's default shape skins or toolbar icon set as a signature look to copy.
- Onboarding modals that block the first shape. Instant empty canvas is the point of a canvas app.
- Marketing feature grids laid over a live diagram session.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the object is a spatial diagram edited by direct manipulation. Wrong for ranked text lists or text-led trust pages.
