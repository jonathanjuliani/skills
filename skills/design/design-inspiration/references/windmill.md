## Taken

- The headline is a testable claim about the audience's pain, then two numbers, then one primary action. Specificity is the design.
- A narrative of one person through the product (join, 1:1, feedback, review) beats a grid of features. Sequence teaches the job.
- Product UI in the hero uses plausible people and roles. Real-looking records, not an illustrated "team".
- Two actions, ranked: Get started for free, then Book a demo.
- A sans for UI. Four weights. Spacing is a regular 4/8. Medium radius on cards.

## Rejected

- The iridescent frame around the product, the named assistant as a mascot, and the handwriting face. Those are identity. The script is decoration, not a second UI family.
- Cookie copy covering the product screenshot. Consent should not sit on the proof.
- A wall of short testimonials of equal weight. Rank or sample; a stack of the same card is not evidence.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. An HR marketing story is not a dense calibration table, even if the product has one later.
