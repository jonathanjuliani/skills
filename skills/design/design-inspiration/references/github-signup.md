## Taken

- One job on screen: create the account. Email, password, username, region, then continue. No competing product tours in the primary column.
- Benefit list sits beside or below the form as reassurance, clearly secondary to the fields that unblock the next step.
- Social continue options are equal alternatives to the form, not a second primary path fighting Create account.
- Legal and cookie choices stay at the edge of the task instead of covering the form before the first field.

## Rejected

- Embedding the full marketing homepage inside signup. Onboarding should remove everything that is not account creation.
- Celebratory ambient motion while typing credentials. Motion that does not explain validation is distraction.
- Multiple equally weighted CTAs (Explore, Pricing, Sign in, Create) in the form column.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing signup or first-run account creation. Wrong for dense issue queues or marketing landings that must persuade before any form.
