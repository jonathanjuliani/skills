## Taken

- One sentence job ("A reference manual for people who design and build software") plus author credit carries the whole first read before any feature grid.
- Table of contents is the product: sections group chapters, and finished chapters show word counts so progress and depth are scannable together.
- Progress is quantified in the open (chapters finished, word count, image count) instead of a vague "coming soon".
- Curiosity-first copy explains what the book is not (not a how-to, not actionable tips) so the wrong buyer self-selects out.
- One primary action (early access purchase) stays ranked above mailing-list secondary paths.

## Rejected

- Award-theatre WebGL as proof of craft. Density and specificity do the work here.
- Equal marketing cards for every chapter. The ranked TOC is the transferable structure.
- Brand illustration style as identity to copy.

## Not applicable here

- Store when selling or presenting a dense learning product for builders. Wrong for operator market scanners or cinematic agency portfolios alone.
