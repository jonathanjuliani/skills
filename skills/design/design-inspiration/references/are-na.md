## Taken

- The product is explained as verbs (Capture, Arrange, Search, Connect, Network), each one job, so the mental model of channels and blocks lands before signup.
- Live search examples rotate concrete queries ("spatial discontinuity", "1980s advertisements") so the collection metaphor is demonstrated, not just claimed.
- Anti-algorithm positioning is explicit: no ads, no personalized recommendations, network you cultivate yourself. Constraint is the product story.
- Primary actions stay Sign up and Download the app. Log in is secondary.
- Long-form sections (business model, API, "the secret") treat readers as peers, which fits an editorial tool audience.

## Rejected

- Copying the soft editorial voice as a universal tone. It fits Are.na's audience; an ops tool needs different plainness.
- Wordmark and type choices as identity.
- Turning the five verbs into five equal marketing cards with icons. The transferable idea is named jobs, not icon grids.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing collection, research, or channel-based products. Wrong for dense financial tables or hardware checkout alone.
