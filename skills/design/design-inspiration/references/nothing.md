## Taken

- Each block is one object and one capability ("Capture what you hear", "Stay in the moment"). Photography of the device does the selling; the claim is short enough to sit on the image.
- Products are ranked as a stack, not a wall of equal cards. Discover is repeated as the one next step per object.
- Imagery is full-bleed and still enough to inspect. Motion, when it appears, is the object or a glyph, not a gradient standing in for the product.
- Geo and cookie chrome covering first paint is a live miss. The object should be the first thing that works.

## Rejected

- The pixel wordmark, yellow field, and specific product photography. Identity.
- A locale chooser on first paint. It fights the scanning the page is built for, the same miss already recorded on other commerce homes.
- Full-bleed colour fields used when the object is not on screen. When the device is missing, the block has nothing to say.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing physical products with photography as proof. Wrong for text-led infrastructure pages or dense operator tables.
