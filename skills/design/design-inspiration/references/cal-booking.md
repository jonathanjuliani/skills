## Taken

- One job on screen: pick a duration (from the profile) then a day and slot. No competing product marketing in the booker.
- Event meta (host, length, location like Google Meet, timezone) stays visible while the calendar is the interactive centre.
- Timezone control is explicit so the booker does not silently fail across regions.
- Month navigation and disabled past days reduce illegal choices instead of erroring after submit.
- Progressive disclosure: profile lists event types first; the booker appears for one chosen event.

## Rejected

- Embedding the full Cal.com marketing site inside the booker. Focused task pages should remove everything but the booking.
- Brand illustration or ambient motion during slot selection. Motion that does not explain availability is noise.
- Multiple primary CTAs (Subscribe, Learn more) beside the calendar.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing checkout-like or booking single-task flows. Wrong for dense operator tables or multi-product marketing homes.
