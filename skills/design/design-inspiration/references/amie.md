## Taken

- Honest comparison copy ("27 meeting notes apps") reframes the category before listing features.
- Product proof is timeline-shaped (today, day 3, day 7) so the buyer can imagine adoption, not only features.
- Replaces lists (Fireflies, Otter, Things) make the job-to-be-done explicit without a feature matrix.
- Integrations are ranked as export destinations for an existing system of record, not as equal partner logos.
- Specific chat actions ("I'm sick, move everything to Thursday") beat generic AI promises.

## Rejected

- Notch UI chrome and brand mascot as signature identity to copy.
- Equal feature cards for every integration. Ranking by outcome transfers.
- Dark-only theatrical heroes for every productivity tool.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing AI productivity startups with workflow adoption stories. Wrong for dense support tables or public-sector directories alone.
