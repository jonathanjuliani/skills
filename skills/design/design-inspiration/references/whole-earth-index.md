## Taken

- The archive is the product. Numbered series (①–⑥) with short provenance paragraphs beat a search box as the first orientation.
- Issue links are dated and dense on purpose. Scanning by season and year is the job; the page does not pretend to be a marketing funnel.
- Chrome stays tiny (Info, Support, Redux). Almost all attention goes to the collection.
- Copy explains what each series was, not what the site wants you to do next. Editorial restraint matches the material.
- One memorable slogan closes the page ("access to tools, ideas, and practices") instead of a second CTA competing with the archive.

## Rejected

- Turning the cover-grid aesthetic into a SaaS hero collage. The density works because the objects are real issues, not feature cards.
- Adding a primary "Sign up" next to the index. This surface is browse-first; conversion would fight the read.
- Treating the circled numbers as a cute brand system to reuse. They are section markers for a catalog family.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the surface is an editorial or archival index and the audience is scanning a large set. Wrong for conversion landings or dense operator tables that need filters and sticky headers.
