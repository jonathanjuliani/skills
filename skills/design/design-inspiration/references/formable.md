## Taken

- The product is the proof. Hero pairs a short claim with tilted UI windows that show real revision, comment, and signature states, not abstract illustrations.
- One primary action in chrome and hero (Sign up / Book a demo). Log in stays visibly secondary.
- Capability sections are verbs of the job (Write, Negotiate, E-Signing, Tracking), so navigation language matches the work the buyer does.
- Colour stays mostly neutral. Hue is reserved for small UI state in the mock (checkbox, active chrome), which keeps a dense legal product readable.
- Copy names the failure mode of the status quo ("separate apps", "email chains") before the feature, so each section answers a known pain.

## Rejected

- The wordmark and exact button black as identity to reuse.
- Slanted overlapping cards as a signature composition. The transferable idea is "show the real editor", not the isometric tilt.
- Equal feature walls without ranking. Their page ranks the workflow; copying a flat grid of six capabilities would undo that.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the surface is product-led SaaS marketing for a tool buyers can recognise in a screenshot. Wrong for public-sector forms or text-only trust pages where a product canvas would look like theatre.
