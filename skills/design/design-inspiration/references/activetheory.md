## Taken

- The image is a 3D mark, not a photograph of a website. A rotating object in a particle field is the proof of craft, with "scroll down" as the only next step.
- Audio is opt-in. Toggle and track controls sit in the chrome, so sound does not start itself on a first visit.
- Native page scroll does not move this world. Scroll position is owned by the canvas, which is why image motion here is high complexity: the picture is the site.
- Cookie copy on the live read was not a usable control. Consent that cannot be hit is a miss; the object should not trap the document.

## Rejected

- The 3D letterform, the particle ribbon, and the studio mark. Signature world.
- Ambient particles that never become work. A field that only shimmers is decoration, not evidence.
- Autoplaying audio with the scene. The toggle exists because a campaign world that also shouts is the failure to avoid.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a campaign or studio page must prove 3D image craft as the first paint. Wrong for product UI, editorial archives, or any audience that cannot wait on a canvas.
