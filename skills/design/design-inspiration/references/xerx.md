## Taken

- One line of taste ("We make digital things with intention") sets the filter before services.
- Five disciplines are numbered and outcome-led, then folded into one studio rather than five microsites.
- Case picks are few and concrete (product launched, Product Hunt rank, pipeline described) instead of a logo wall.
- Process is four short verbs (listen, frame, make, ship and sustain) with one sentence each.
- Direct-line positioning (no account managers) is a constraint buyers can evaluate.

## Rejected

- Exact wordmark and Innsbruck brand colour as identity to reuse.
- Cinematic WebGL as proof of a serious studio. Named shipped work transfers better.
- Equal testimonial cards without ranking. A few specific quotes beat a carousel of peers.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing indie product studios to founders. Wrong for dense browser-support tables or enterprise CNAPP proof pages alone.
