## Taken

- The canvas is the first paint. No marketing wall; tools appear around an empty infinite board ready for marks.
- Tool palette stays compact (select, hand, draw, erase, shapes, text, image, frame) so the spatial artifact keeps most of the viewport.
- Style controls (colour, stroke, fill, size) dock beside the canvas and apply to the selection, which is the canvas-app convention.
- Collaboration ask is honest: Sign in to share. Anonymous drawing works; sharing is the gate, not browsing.
- Zoom and undo live at the edges, never competing with the board as a second hero.

## Rejected

- Treating the specific tool icon set or blue selection chrome as a signature to copy.
- Filling empty canvas with onboarding modals before the first stroke. Instant empty board is the point.
- Marketing feature grids on top of a live canvas session.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the surface is a spatial canvas and the object is drawn or arranged freely. Wrong for ranked tables or text-only trust pages.
