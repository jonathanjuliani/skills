## Taken

- A real interface with plausible loan data is the hero visual. For this buyer, a table of collateral is more persuasive than an illustration of "lending".
- Two actions, ranked: Get started, then Explore. They are not twins.
- The headline names the job (build B2B lending products). The subhead names the product types. A procurement reader can match themselves in one pass.
- Later sections are named capabilities, each with a next step, rather than three equal feature cards with icons.
- A display sans plus a mono. Three weights. Spacing is 4/8 at large marketing steps, not dense-UI steps. Cards use a medium radius; pills are for chips.

## Rejected

- The lime product-frame hue and the hashed build-font names in the scrape. Identity, and the hue is doing decoration rather than state.
- A logo row as the first proof after the hero. Named customer stories later are the evidence; the logos are not.
- Decorative line-art beside the screenshot. The screenshot already did the work.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. This is marketing for a dense lending OS, not the OS itself.
