## Taken

- The wait is an explicit start. "Click to start" keeps an expensive 3D world from autoplaying, so loading is a consent gate rather than a spinner the user did not ask for.
- The image is the world. After start, the portfolio is a driveable scene, which is why photography here is 3D evidence of craft, not a screenshot of someone else's UI.
- Chrome is almost absent. Work is places in the scene, so a hiring scan judges by what can be driven, not by a case-study grid.
- First paint before the click was dark. Transfer the opt-in start, not a void. Reduced motion and a still fallback matter more here than on a CSS loader.

## Rejected

- The car, the playground layout, and the personal wordmark. Signature world.
- Autoplaying physics on arrival. A world that starts itself is the failure the click is avoiding.
- Treating a playable scene as a layout for a product app. This is a portfolio where presence is the product; a records list is not.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a portfolio or campaign must prove 3D craft as the image, with a start the user chooses. Wrong for marketing that has to convert on first paint, and for any operator tool.
