## Taken

- The headline is a sentence that can be read in any order of its parts. Words restack ("research", "products", "safety", "frontier") while a still paragraph on the right holds the claim, so kinetic type never strands the meaning.
- Skip links for main content and footer. A type-led lab page that offers skip is treating attention as optional.
- One primary action (Try Claude), with research and policy as navigation rather than competing heroes.
- Releases below the hero are dated and named. Specificity about models beats a looping slogan.

## Rejected

- The wordmark, the circular hero film, and the exact restack choreography. Those are identity.
- Rearranging type on a dense or regulated surface. This audience is reading a lab's posture; operators scanning a table are not.
- Cookie or consent chrome on first paint. The type-led centre should be the first thing that works.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a text-led marketing page wants light kinetic type that still reads as a sentence. Wrong for product UI, checkout, or any audience that needs the whole claim instantly.
