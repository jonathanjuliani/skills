## Taken

- List and detail share one grammar: rank, title, source, points, author, age, comment count. Selecting a story opens the thread without inventing a second visual language.
- Density is extreme on purpose. One line per story maximises how many items an expert can triage before choosing.
- Navigation names places (new, past, comments, ask, show, jobs) rather than features, so the list stays the centre.
- Metadata that decides click-worthiness (comment count, age, domain) sits on the same row as the title.
- Almost no chrome. The absence of cards and hero imagery is the design decision.

## Rejected

- Restyling HN's orange identity or monospace habit as a brand to import.
- Replacing the single-line row with equal cards. Cards slow triage for this audience.
- Infinite marketing modules above the list. The feed is the product.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the surface is a high-density list that opens into threaded detail. Wrong for marketing heroes or canvas editors.
