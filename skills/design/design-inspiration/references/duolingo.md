## Taken

- The wait is a character. A mascot on a phone mock idles, reacts, and invites while the headline and two ranked actions stay still, so loading still looks like the product.
- State is readable without the animation. "Get started" and "I already have an account" work if motion is off. The character explains mood, it does not gate the task.
- Course chips sit as a scannable row. The page does not make you wait through a film before you can pick a language.
- One primary, one secondary. Habit products that rank start above return keep the first-time path obvious.

## Rejected

- The owl, the lime brand, and the specific character acting. Identity.
- A mascot that loops forever on an operator tool. This audience is starting a habit; a table of records is not.
- Forcing every wait state to be a character. Reuse "the wait is still the product", not a creature.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a consumer product wants wait and idle to feel like coaching. Wrong for checkout, dense tools, or any surface where a character would hide the task.
