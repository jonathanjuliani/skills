## Taken

- The headline stays still and large. "Digital design and development agency" plus one line of job is the claim; kinetic energy is reserved for the work under it, so type does not fight the sentence.
- Work is a moving collage of product screens around one featured case. The type explains who they are; the collage explains what they have shipped.
- Later sections return to still type. Services and FAQ are readable blocks, which is how a motion-led hero can still end in a document a buyer can scan.
- First paint was a white wait, then the headline. Transfer a readable claim as soon as type exists, not a void while the collage boots.

## Rejected

- The specific case screens, the wordmark, and a looping collage as wallpaper. Signature reel.
- Split or morphing type on the hero sentence. Their craft here is stillness of the claim, not kinetic letterforms.
- Collage motion on a dense product surface. This audience is hiring a studio; an operator table does not need orbiting screens.

## Not applicable here

- Store when studio marketing needs a loud still headline and moving work as proof. Wrong for tools, checkout, or text-led trust pages without cases to show.
