## Taken

- A short self-definition and a "Now" line establish who is speaking before the feed.
- Recent notes are title-first with a one-sentence lede and light metadata (date, read time).
- Topics stay close to shipped practice (tokens, contrast, debt, WebMCP) so the blog proves craft.
- Layout stays text-led and calm; hierarchy comes from type and space, not cards.
- Continuity between site systems and posts (the site is the case study) makes the archive credible.

## Rejected

- WebGL portfolio theatre as the default personal site. Writing density is the signal here.
- Equal project cards competing with notes. The notes list is the primary surface.
- Signature illustration systems or brand hues to reuse.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing personal or engineer blogs. Wrong for dense financial market tables or enterprise security proof pages alone.
