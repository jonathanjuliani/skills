## Taken

- One operating model claim (context graph across code, cloud, runtime) organizes the page before tool laundry lists.
- Workflows are sequenced (attack surface, deep analysis, fix at scale, detect and block) so scanners follow a path rather than a wall of capabilities.
- Analyst and review proof sits near claims with numbers (review counts, ratings) instead of logo rows alone.
- Outcome blocks (speed, impact, simplicity) use short quotes with role and title, then one metric.
- Separate audiences (code, cloud, defend) are named without building three competing sites.

## Rejected

- Exact brand blues and wordmark as identity to reuse.
- Equal three-agent cards as the default structure for every platform. The ranked exposure-to-fix path is what transfers.
- Cinematic WebGL as a substitute for a clear security story.

## Not applicable here

- Store when marketing enterprise security platforms with dense proof. Wrong for quiet personal blogs or single-task checkout flows.
