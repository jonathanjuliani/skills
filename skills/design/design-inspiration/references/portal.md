## Taken

- One primary action (Join beta), repeated. The page does not split the visitor between two equal conversions.
- The product is framed as a place for the work (a digital home for a project), not as a list of invoice features. Structure follows that idea: proposal, pay, deliver.
- Copy names the jobs (proposals, payments, subscriptions, contracts) rather than a mood.
- A display face on the hero, a sans for UI, three weights. Spacing is 4/8 with large section jumps. Radius is generous everywhere; that is marketing chrome, and here it also sits on an empty frame.

## Rejected

- Near-white type on a near-white hero. Contrast failure. Restraint is not the same as disappearing.
- An empty rounded frame standing in for the product. A blank panel is a placeholder, not proof.
- Soft particle decoration in the hero. Texture without hierarchy.
- The display face. Identity. Generous radius on an empty frame is not a component scale.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. A freelance marketing page is the wrong reference for an internal ops table, even if the product later contains one.
