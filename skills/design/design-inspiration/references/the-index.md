## Taken

- Directory density wins: name, city, year, and practice tags in a scannable stack without card chrome.
- Latest and shuffle surfaces create two entry modes (recency vs discovery) without a second site.
- Metadata stays secondary to the studio name so scanning stays fast.
- Restraint in motion and colour keeps the index usable for long sessions.
- Selection taste is the product; the UI gets out of the way of the list.

## Rejected

- Award-theatre motion on a directory. Scan speed is the craft.
- Equal big preview cards for every studio. Density of names is the point.
- Brand identity of featured studios as something to reuse.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing curated directories or indexes. Wrong for single-product cinematic landings or focused checkout alone.
