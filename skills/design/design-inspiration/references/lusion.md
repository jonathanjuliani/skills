## Taken

- The wait is a progress bar that becomes the scene. Loading explains that a world is arriving, then hands off to the same canvas, so the door and the destination share one timeline.
- After the bar, the page is a 3D field with an instruction to scroll. The transition is complete when the user can move, not when a logo finishes spinning.
- First paint was a void, then the bar. Transfer the honest wait, not the black. A hiring scan should still know a document is coming.
- WebGL and the page are one state. Scroll position equals where you are in the world, which is why this wait is high complexity rather than a CSS spinner.

## Rejected

- The plus-shaped 3D field and the studio wordmark. Signature world, not a convention.
- Black first paint as a studio default. A progress bar that never appears leaves the user with a broken tab.
- Copying a named load-into-WebGL sequence into a product app. This audience is hiring presence; operators do not wait on a scene to triage records.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a brand or studio page must wait on a 3D world and then keep that world as the site. Wrong for SaaS chrome, checkout, or any surface that has to be a document on first paint.
