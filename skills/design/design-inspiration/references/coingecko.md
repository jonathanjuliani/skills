## Taken

- Ranked rows by market cap are the first answer. Name, price, and change read in one scan before any article content.
- Category and ecosystem chips (All, Highlights, Base, Categories) narrow the set without opening a separate page.
- Pagination and page-size controls admit the list is long; density is deliberate, not accidental overflow.
- Highlight strips (gainers, trending) sit beside the main table so secondary scans do not replace the primary ranking.
- Customize and Filter are available but do not bury the default table under empty chrome.

## Rejected

- Carousel market summaries as a substitute for the table. They are flavour; the ranked list is the tool.
- Emoji-heavy category labels as a brand language to copy.
- Treating infinite promotional tiles as required density. Extra modules compete with comparison.

## Not applicable here

- Store when buyers need a dense, filterable market or asset table. Wrong for single-task booking flows or editorial long-form.
