## Taken

- The page is the sample. The headline itself enters, swaps, and settles, so kinetic type is evidence of the library rather than a screenshot of someone else's site.
- Capabilities are named as jobs (Scroll, SVG, Text, UI), each with one line. Ranking by job beats a plugin matrix.
- Skip to main content is present. A motion-led marketing page that still offers a skip is taking reduced attention seriously.
- First paint still has a claim once the entrance finishes. The type sequence is short enough that a paused or reduced-motion visitor is not left with an empty hero.

## Rejected

- The wordmark, lime banner, and named plugin list as identity to reuse.
- Split-text on every heading. One kinetic line as proof transfers; a site where every sentence performs does not.
- Implementation recipes (plugin names, durations). Record "the headline is the demo", not a timeline to copy.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a marketing page needs to prove motion craft in type. Wrong for dense tools, checkout, or public-sector services where kinetic headlines fight scanning.
