## Taken

- The ranking table is the product. Search, chain filters, column toggles, and period radios (1d / 7d / 1m) sit above rows so comparison stays in one place.
- Sortable metric headers (TVL, Market Cap, Fees, Revenue) let operators change the question without leaving the list.
- Child-protocol expanders keep parent rows dense while still exposing structure on demand.
- Export (.csv) and embed sit next to the chart, treating data as something to take away, not only to admire.
- Chrome stays secondary to the table. Marketing banners can dismiss; the rankings remain the job.

## Rejected

- Newsletter modals and premium upsells as the primary layout. They interrupt comparison; the table discipline is what transfers.
- Logo-heavy protocol branding as identity to reuse.
- Equal KPI cards replacing a sortable table. Cards hide rank; tables make rank the point.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the surface is an operator comparison table. Wrong for text-led marketing or canvas tools where rows are not the job.
