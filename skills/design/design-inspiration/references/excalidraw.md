## Taken

- Opens straight into the board with shapes, draw, text, sticky note, and eraser named for the job.
- Library and live collaboration are available without blocking the first shape.
- Zoom, undo, and redo sit with canvas actions, keeping the spatial surface dominant.
- Help and open/save are peripheral; the default state is "ready to draw", not "ready to read a tour".
- Encryption messaging for collaboration states a trust constraint in one line when it matters, not as a homepage essay.

## Rejected

- The rough hand-drawn aesthetic as a mandatory look. It is their signature; the transferable idea is canvas-first chrome.
- Forcing sticky notes as the primary object on every canvas product. They fit brainstorming here; not every spatial tool.
- Sign-up walls before a single stroke.

## Not applicable here

- Store when comparing canvas tool chrome and first-run emptiness. Wrong for list-detail feeds or pricing-forward memberships.
