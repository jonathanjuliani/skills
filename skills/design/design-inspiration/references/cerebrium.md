## Taken

- The body compares cold-start times with named alternatives. A latency claim without a comparison is an adjective; a comparison is evidence.
- Use cases are named workloads (voice agent, podcast transcription), which is how this audience maps themselves.
- Security and compliance are a list of constraints (residency, isolation, uptime), not a lock icon. Constraints outrank atmosphere for infra buyers.
- A display grotesque plus a sans plus a mono. Spacing is 4/8 mixed with computed fractions from motion. Mixed small radii.

## Rejected

- Magenta on a dark star-field hero, and the trial typeface names. Brand identity, and it is close to the generated purple-to-dark default.
- Fractional spacing leftover from animation. Not a scale.
- A privacy modal covering the headline. First paint should be the claim.
- A branded loader as the only first frame. Delay without content is not craft.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. Infra marketing with latency tables is useful; the magenta hero is not a theme.
