## Taken

- The product screenshot is a cockpit with plausible money in it. This audience needs to believe the numbers will show up on day one.
- Copy is specific about the mess it replaces (invoices in one tool, clients in a spreadsheet). The claim is a job, not an adjective.
- One primary action (Start your trial), with trial terms stated under it. Secondary banners (founding member, e-signatures) are quieter.
- Display type is restrained in count even when the voice is editorial. One display face plus a sans. The dashboard, not extra weights, carries the second look.
- Spacing is a 4/8 base with many one-offs. Soft radius. Shadows in the scrape are the glow under the product, not elevation.

## Rejected

- The blue glow under the product shot. Texture standing in for hierarchy, and it fades the UI it is meant to prove.
- The wordmark and the named families (five in the scrape, including fallbacks). Keep at most one display and one sans, and substitute both.
- An intro animation that hides the page. First paint should be the claim, not a progress bar.

## Not applicable here

- Store-only pass. Apply when a product is named. A freelancer marketing page is not a brief for a bank-grade ledger UI.
