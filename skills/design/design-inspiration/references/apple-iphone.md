## Taken

- The family is a strip of real objects, then one product gets a film. Photography and video of the device do the selling; chrome is almost absent.
- The film has a pause control. Cinematic media that the user can stop is the difference between a product story and a trap.
- Two actions per hero, ranked: learn more, then view pricing. They are not equal.
- Type is a handful of sizes. The product name is the loudest thing on the block; the rest stays quiet so the object can move.

## Rejected

- The product photography, the wordmark, and the system typeface. Identity.
- Pill CTAs as a commerce signature. Reuse ranked actions, not the capsule.
- Autoplaying a hero film with no pause on a dense or regulated surface. This audience came to compare devices; operators did not.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a product family page uses scroll and media to rank one object at a time. Wrong for a records list, a form, or a text-led trust page without a physical product.
