## Taken

- The entrance is a short greeting sequence (hello in several languages) then a still portrait. The preloader is the transition; it ends, and the page becomes a photograph plus a job title.
- The name is a scrolling line over the portrait. Kinetic type is reserved for identity of the person, not for every heading. Work below is named projects with year and role.
- Chrome is almost absent: Work, About, Contact. A hiring scan does not need a studio nav soup.
- The greeting is skippable in practice because it is short. A longer multilingual loop would hide the portrait the page is built to show.

## Rejected

- The portrait, the exact greeting list, and the oversized scrolling name. Signature choreography.
- A dark void as first paint. The live read started black; the transferable craft is a short door, then a still.
- Copying page-wipe transitions into a product app. This audience is scanning a person, not triaging records.

## Not applicable here

- Store when a portfolio or brand page wants a short entrance then still photography. Wrong for dense tools, forms, or marketing that must be readable on first paint without a door.
