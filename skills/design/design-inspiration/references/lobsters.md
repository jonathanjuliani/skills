## Taken

- List rows combine score, title, domain, tags, author, and time so a reader can triage before opening the thread.
- Tags are first-class filters on the same surface as the feed, which teaches the community's topics without a separate taxonomy page.
- Detail is the comment thread, not a redesigned article template. List and detail stay one product.
- Chrome is thin: Active, Recent, Comments, Search. Places to go, not feature marketing.

## Rejected

- Copying Lobsters' colour or score glyph as identity.
- Inflating rows into card grids. This audience is scanning peers' links, not browsing a storefront.
- Scroll-triggered entrance on stories. Instant paint matches how people reload a feed.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing a tagged discussion list that opens into threads. Wrong for checkout flows or cinematic marketing.
