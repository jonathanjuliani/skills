## Taken

- Once in the editor, menus and tools (File, Edit, Image, Layer, Select, Filter) frame a document canvas. The spatial file is the centre; chrome is application chrome.
- Tool density matches the job: professional editors expect many commands near the canvas, not a marketing-simplified three-button strip.
- Documents open into the board without a forced product tour. Local editing is the promise; the UI starts ready to mark pixels.
- Panels and menus support the active document rather than replacing it with promotional modules mid-session.

## Rejected

- Importing the marketing homepage (feature sections, repeated Start CTAs) as if it were the editor. Capture the canvas app, not the brochure.
- Treating Photopea's exact menu labels or ad placements as craft to reuse. Ads are their monetisation, not a pattern to copy.
- Ambient motion on tool switches. Feedback should follow the edit, not decorate the chrome.

## Not applicable here

- Store when designing a dense image or document canvas. Wrong for checkout, list-detail feeds, or cinematic product marketing.
