## Taken

- Text carries the product: what Resend is for, when to use it, and how to call it, before visual theatre.
- Navigation is a map of jobs (CLI, MCP, Skills, Docs, Pricing, Blog) so a technical buyer can jump without a hero animation.
- Constraints are stated early (opted-in recipients, no inbox scraping), which builds trust for API buyers.
- Markdown and machine-readable indexes (llms.txt, OpenAPI) treat agents as first-class readers.
- Pricing and legal are linked as peers to docs, not buried only in the footer.

## Rejected

- Requiring a cinematic hero for every developer tool. This surface wins by clarity of routes.
- Brand illustration systems as identity to copy.
- Equal marketing cards for every SDK. A list of official libraries is enough.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the brief is text-led developer documentation entry. Wrong for consumer threat marketing or dense market scanners.
