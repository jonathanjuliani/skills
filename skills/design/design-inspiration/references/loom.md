## Taken

- Hero pairs a short claim with the product in use (record / cancel / share controls), off-centre energy without a second equal CTA fight.
- One primary action repeats (Get Loom for free). Install extension and Learn more stay secondary.
- Use-case panels (Sales, Engineering, Support, Design) are unequal jobs, each with one outcome sentence, not four identical feature cards.
- Feature list is concrete (trim, stitch, transcripts, privacy controls) after the hero proves the product exists.
- Numbers that matter to buyers appear as scale proof (companies, languages) beside stories, not as a floating stat strip in the first viewport alone.

## Rejected

- Cookie and preference chrome as part of the composition.
- Wordmark and purple accents as identity to reuse.
- Equal bento tiles for every feature. Their use-case section ranks jobs; a flat icon row would undo that.

## Not applicable here

- Store when the landing needs asymmetric product proof plus ranked use cases. Wrong for archival indexes or canvas-only apps with no marketing layer.
