# Gallery sources

Where to discover categories and candidate projects. The gallery is a finder; the capture target is always the live project URL. Do not scrape a gallery into a dataset, and do not capture the gallery's own marketing page as if it were the reference.

Confirm a small subset per session. Do not crawl every row below in one pass.

## Out of the default batch

Leave these out of automatic curation unless the user names them:

- **Dribbble / Behance:** often concept work, not a shipped site you can read as a product.
- **Muzli and similar aggregators:** high noise; unstable as a category or example source.

## What to take from a gallery page

- Category, tag, collection, or filter names the source uses.
- Link (or clear identity) of the winning or featured *project*.
- Enough context to judge surface and audience before you open the project.

## What not to take

- The gallery's chrome, brand, or catalog layout as a capture.
- Screenshots or thumbnails into the store (session viewing only).
- A full mirror of winners lists. Pick a few per confirmed category.
